F450 Drone Build
I wanted a hands on project to keep me off my phone this summer so I decided to build a drone.

At first I was planning to design and 3D print my own frame in Fusion 360. I spent some time researching parts, learning how all the electronics connect together, and making a budget spreadsheet so I could stay under the $400 limit.

After testing a few frame designs I realised it probably wouldnt be strong enough and would be kinda annoying to print, so I decided to switch to a pre made F450 frame instead. - https://cdn.hackclub.com/019e7489-814b-72ef-93f0-138aafcbfc2b/Screenshot%202026-05-28%20at%205.44.51%E2%80%AFPM.png

After that I focused on picking parts, making a wiring diagram, and checking that everything would work together. I also found out that my receiver wasnt compatible with my flight controller, so I had to find a different one. I did some rough calculations and estimated that the drone should get around 5 to 6 minutes of flight time.

The final cost came out to $391.94 which kept the project under budget.
